Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that generates new content, such as text, images, music, or videos, based on a given prompt or input. It uses machine learning algorithms to create new and original content that is often indistinguishable from human-created content.

Agentic AI is a type of artificial intelligence that is designed to take actions on behalf of a user or organization. It is a form of autonomous AI that can make decisions and take actions without human intervention.

Agentic AI is a rapidly evolving field that has the potential to revolutionize many industries and aspects of our lives. It is being used in applications such as personal assistants, autonomous vehicles, and robots.
