Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that generates new content, such as text, images, music, or videos, based on a given prompt or input. It uses machine learning algorithms to create new and original content that is often indistinguishable from human-created content. Generative AI has a wide range of applications, including art, design, music, and even healthcare. Some of the top competitors in the Generative AI space include Google, Microsoft, and NVIDIA. Generative AI is being used in various industries, including entertainment, education, and marketing.
