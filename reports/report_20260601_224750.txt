Agentic AI refers to a type of artificial intelligence that is capable of taking initiative, making decisions, and acting independently. It is a more advanced form of AI that is designed to be proactive and autonomous, rather than simply reactive. Agentic AI systems are able to learn from their environment, adapt to new situations, and make decisions based on their own goals and motivations. This type of AI has the potential to revolutionize many industries, including healthcare, finance, and transportation. However, it also raises important questions about accountability, ethics, and the potential risks of creating autonomous systems that are capable of making decisions on their own.
